Time, kept the slow way.

INSIDE EVERY AURUM

Built by hand, checked by eye

01

The Calibre

Our in-house A-1962 movement holds 214 parts and a 72 hour reserve. Each one is regulated in five positions before it ever meets a case.

02

The Case

Solid 18k gold, milled from a single billet and polished by one pair of hands from first cut to final shine. No two polishes are ever identical.

03

The Finish

Gold markers are applied by hand under magnification, the dial is lacquered in nine coats, and a sapphire crystal seals it all against the world.
